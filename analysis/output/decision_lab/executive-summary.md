# Help a reviewer find defensible evidence with minimal maintenance burden

Prioritize role-specific navigation and short decision summaries. Judge the site by evidence discoverability and correctness, not invented interview conversions or visitor counts.

**Observed evidence:** Ten self-directed case studies are linked with interactive tools and validated calculations. Local optional events are in-memory only; there is no production visitor-retention dataset.

**Decision owner:** Portfolio owner (proposed role, not a client).

**Base planning case:** Static case-study index. This is conditional on the assumptions, not deployment approval.

| Alternative | Economic cost | Cash outlay | Reviewer hours | Passes risk and capacity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Static case-study index | $805 | $205 | 10.0 | True |
| Guided role navigation with concise evidence | $1,567 | $1,207 | 6.0 | True |
| Complex personalized tracking stack | $6,320 | $5,120 | 20.0 | False |

Economic cost includes valued staff capacity and scenario incident losses. Cash outlay excludes salaried capacity and contingent losses. These are not realized savings.

**Sensitivity across 27 equally enumerated scenarios:** Static case-study index: 18, NO FEASIBLE OPTION: 9. Grid counts are not probabilities.

**Synthetic product experiment:** HOLD. Primary effect 7.0%; 95% interval [3.2%, 10.8%]. Guardrail effect 1.5%; interval [0.8%, 2.3%]. These numbers test the decision logic, not product performance.

**Priority order:**

1. Lead each role path with three relevant projects and their actual evidence limits.
2. Keep contact URLs clean and keyboard navigation usable.
3. Use opt-in local QA to validate event mechanics; do not introduce visitor tracking just to fill an analytics chart.

**What would reverse the recommendation:** If added navigation raises maintenance cost without reducing task difficulty, keep the simpler index. Any incorrect claim or privacy violation blocks release.

**Remaining uncertainty:** Synthetic repeat visits cannot measure recruiter interest. The site creates presentation value; avoided reviewer minutes are not cash revenue.
