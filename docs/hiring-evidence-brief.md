# Trustworthy measurement for commercial and product decisions

Independent, AI-assisted portfolio work by Harshit Malik. Reviewed October 14, 2026. This brief connects business responsibilities to inspectable artifacts; it is not an employer endorsement or an assessment of independently demonstrated personal proficiency.

## The problem this work addresses

When reports disagree, teams can prioritize the wrong change. When an experiment appears positive, incomplete measurement or adverse effects can still make rollout a poor decision. This portfolio connects source reconciliation, metric definitions, commercial trade-offs, experiment interpretation and report recovery in one commerce case.

## A result a reviewer can trace

The [assignment-to-decision investigation](../projects/commerce-conversion/experiment/ledger/index.html) adds an integrated three-role exercise. SQL retains every assigned visitor; the product analysis diagnoses selection from unequal exposure; the business record evaluates a denominator change and its conditional financial consequence. Inputs are explicitly synthetic. The technical contract passes while the release decision remains blocked by unverified design. This distinction demonstrates evidence handling rather than inventing a successful experiment.

The [historical manifest](../projects/commerce-conversion/reporting/output/full/manifest.json) records 2,756,101 raw rows, 460 collapsed duplicates, 2,755,641 distinct events, 22,457 transaction item rows and 17,672 orders. Twelve reconciliation checks pass in that recorded run. The report covers 139 observed UTC dates in 2015. Source and output hashes support reproducibility; they are not external validation.

The distinction between item rows and orders is a concrete analytical decision. So is refusing to sum daily visitors into period-unique visitors. Neither finding demonstrates financial savings: the source has no prices or margins. The Retailrocket-derived artifacts retain their documented noncommercial/share-alike licensing limits.

## A useful first assignment

For a suitable employed role, agree on one recurring decision and its accountable owner. Define the metric and source contracts, reconcile a baseline, deliver a repeatable report and document how to handle exceptions. Acceptance would require agreed business definitions, reproducible totals, visible failure behavior and a handoff the owner can follow. Timing depends on access and scope; no delivery date, savings amount or employment eligibility is assumed here.

## Evaluate ownership directly

The [three-role review kit](../projects/commerce-conversion/operations/ownership-review.md) supplies a varied synthetic case and an unfilled rubric. Ask for a change to a definition, a correction to an input or a revised business constraint. Evaluate the explanation and the tested response, including acknowledgment of uncertainty. Public reference answers make this practice material rather than a secure assessment. A passing software test is not a passing candidate evaluation.
