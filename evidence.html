Inspect a decision in three minutes

1. Change the definition.

In commerce analytics, switch from 15 to 60 minutes. Compare the ordered cart transition with visitor conversion over seven days. Different denominators answer different business questions.

2. Change the economics.

Set contribution margin to zero. Positive gross revenue cannot pay back a fixed investment without contribution. Then restore the margin and inspect the break-even lift.

3. Try to break the launch case.

In the experiment lab, select “Conversion up, harm up,” then “Broken randomization ratio.” The recommendation changes for distinct reasons. Export a local decision record with all inputs.
