What the numbers mean
Buyer rate is observed buyers divided by observed visitors on the same UTC day. It is not an ordered funnel, attributed conversion or causal treatment effect. Orders have no assumed monetary value.
A closed reporting period may still be restated when late data arrives. Each published version remains stored. The reporting clock is fixed to January 2, 2026 for this reproducible fixture.
What a stakeholder should do
Use the report's original timestamp when a refresh is rejected. Investigate the failure before republishing. Review restated days before treating a movement as customer behavior.
A completeness watermark is an upstream assertion. A production release still requires source ownership, workload validation, security and an agreed correction policy.
