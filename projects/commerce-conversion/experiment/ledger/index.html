2. Explain the business consequence

Assume 10,000 future eligible visitors, $80 per incremental order, 35% contribution margin, $1,000 fixed cost and one incremental order per additional buyer. Applying the all-assigned descriptive difference produces $15,800 modeled net contribution. Applying the selected-population difference produces $58,111.

The $42,311 gap is an illustration of model sensitivity, not money earned or lost. Neither projection is approved: causal validity, future audience comparability, costs and the harm ceiling remain unverified.

3. Separate a passing data check from permission to ship

Log contract: PASSED. Decision engine: INVALID / NOT READY.

The pipeline checks assignment consistency, event identity, cross-arm records, timestamps, duplicate delivery and the seven-day window. It keeps unexposed and immature assignments. It cannot prove that missing events were captured, people were independently randomized, or an analysis plan existed before results were seen.
