Synthetic work sample. All experiments and planning inputs here are illustrative. The historical audience size of 36,141 is reused as a scenario input, not a forecast of future traffic. No observed revenue or causal business impact is claimed.

Each experiment has 27 combinations: three cost levels × three audiences × three margins. A pass fraction is not a probability; the scenarios have no assigned likelihood. Order value stays at an assumed $75. All existing validity and harm gates remain in force.

2. Challenge incomplete outcomes

A missing outcome is not a known non-purchase. Keep every assigned visitor in the denominator and allow each unknown outcome to be either 0 or 1.

Known successes remain 11,000 control and 12,400 treatment. Unknown records are disjoint from these successes; the remaining records are known negatives. This is a separate sensitivity exercise, not a reconstruction of the selected experiment above.

These are identification bounds, not confidence intervals. They show what the supplied incomplete counts permit, without assuming why outcomes are missing. They do not cover sampling uncertainty, cure biased assignment or establish causality. Any unknown outcomes keep this review on hold even if both bounds are positive.

3. Turn the analysis into a decision

Data analyst

Reconcile the known positives, known negatives and unknown population. Validate disjoint counts before calculating. Never silently fill unknown outcomes with zero.

Business analyst

Request credible cost, contribution and audience ranges from proposed owners. Compare lower-bound contribution across the grid before approving the investment case.

Product analyst

Hold when assignment, follow-up, instrumentation or harm checks fail. Investigate missing outcomes before interpreting an apparent lift or recommending rollout.

Recommendation for the clear-gate fixture: request review of the funding assumptions. It is eligible in 23 of 27 combinations, so the result is assumption-sensitive. It is not robust across this chosen grid and is not automatic launch approval.
