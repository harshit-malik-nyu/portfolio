03 / ALLOCATION

What must improve to pay for the change?

The source has no prices or margins. Every dollar below is a planning assumption in USD, not observed revenue. Eligible populations overlap, so opportunities must not be added together.

Editable investment assumptions

One additional order per incremental converting visitor; constant order value and margin; future audience equal in size to this historical eligible group. No guarantee of transportability or lift.

CONDITIONAL SCENARIO

No realized savings or causal revenue gain is claimed. A positive scenario supports investigating a hypothesis, not automatic rollout.

Experiment handoff

Assign

Randomize visitors persistently at their first eligible cart. One assignment per visitor; prevent cross-device contamination where identity permits.

Measure

Primary: any completed order within seven days per assigned visitor. Retain failures and wait for complete follow-up. Prespecify a meaningful lift and stopping rule.

Protect

Instrument errors, latency, cancellations and margin before launch. These guardrails are absent from this dataset and cannot be declared passed.

04 / AUDIT

What the data can and cannot establish.

Reconciliation

Download computed metrics · Download cohorts

Scope

Real historical events from one anonymous commerce site. No device, marketing channel, checkout-step, payment-failure, price or refund fields. Funnel drop-off is not proof of a broken checkout.

Visitors are anonymous identifiers, not verified people. First observed activity is left-censored. Repeated sessions are dependent. The dataset cannot establish current market behavior or experiment results.
