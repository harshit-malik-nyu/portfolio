01 / EXPERIMENT DECISION

A signal is not permission to launch.

Hold rollout

Observed synthetic difference: $100.00 per assigned account. Conservative 95% effect interval: $-262.58 to $300.00.

The exact sharp-null test returns p = 0.002165 (2 of 924 allocations). The separate bounded average-effect interval still includes harm. It uses prespecified $0–$300 bounds on both potential outcomes, complete random assignment and complete follow-up. It is intentionally conservative; these assumptions must be defensible before applying it to real data.

Missing outcomes stop the calculation. Statistical REVIEW never authorizes rollout. No power or real-world effect is inferred from this small fixture.

02 / FINANCIAL CONTROL

One credit. One refund. No double counting.

Credit issued, refund pending

$100 collected − $20 credit leaves $80 net billed, $100 cash and a $20 customer credit. Receivable: $0.

Refund completed

$80 net billed, $80 net cash, $0 receivable and $0 customer credit. Neither adjustment changes contracted MRR.

These controls cover one invoice, reject duplicate or excessive adjustments and exclude events after cutoff. Proration, credit reversals, tax and accounting recognition remain outside scope.
