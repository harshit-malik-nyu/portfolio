01 / FINANCIAL CONTROL

Three numbers. Different meanings.

Contracted MRR is the month-end subscription run rate, not GAAP revenue. Collections are payments attached to the selected month's invoices received by the snapshot date—not cash received during that month.

Follow the reconciliation

Contracted MRR − billing gap − collection gap = collected cash. A negative billing gap means overbilling.

Activation contract

Activation is a fixture flag representing a completed setup within 14 days. Usage measures active days per account-month. Neither field is a randomized treatment.

What this cannot establish

Small synthetic cohorts cannot establish a causal activation effect or predict a real customer's churn. Test the measurement system; do not interpret generated patterns as market evidence.

03 / BUDGET ALLOCATION

Choose a pilot. See what must be true.

Compare mutually exclusive pilots on modeled incremental cash over 90 days, after variable delivery costs and pilot spend. These are planning assumptions—not estimated treatment effects. Pools are fixed to all September accounts.

Set the constraints

0× means no incremental benefit; 2× doubles each assumed effect. Pilot costs include staff time; hours constrain capacity and are not charged twice.

Economics, overlap and uncertainty

Payment recovery targets overdue invoices: 35% assumed incremental recovery, one-time cash, with prior delivery costs treated as sunk. Activation targets unactivated active subscriptions: 12% assumed incremental retention; renewal targets activated subscriptions with fewer than 18 active days: 8% assumed incremental retention. Both retention cases use three months of collected subscription value at 80% contribution margin, assuming immediate effect, no churn thereafter and full collection. These assumptions are optimistic and editable through the effect multiplier.

Payment recovery can overlap with retention targets. The model chooses one pilot; it never adds their benefits. It is a short-term cash objective, not a profit or lifetime-value ranking. Fixed overhead, tax, time value of money and longer-term effects are excluded. Break-even is cost ÷ eligible value after horizon and margin. Negative or infeasible cases lead to a hold decision.

Evidence needed before rollout

Randomize eligible accounts to treatment or usual process before contact. Prespecify 90-day incremental collected contribution per assigned account as the primary outcome; report uncertainty, delivery cost, churn and complaint guardrails. Block repeat assignment across overlapping interventions. Do not stop early on a favorable result. Sixty synthetic accounts support a demonstration, not a powered effectiveness claim.
