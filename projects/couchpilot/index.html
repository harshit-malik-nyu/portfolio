03 / PRODUCT JUDGMENT
Useful AI knows
when to hold.
The product must complete the intended task, keep the viewer in control, and fail visibly. A plausible answer is insufficient.
Read the product brief →
01
Ground every action
Timestamps and explanation cards come from the supplied lesson. The model selects evidence; it cannot invent playback coordinates.
02
Ask before acting
Proposals are reviewed before changing playback or preparing a handoff. Undo brings the viewer back.
03
Measure the whole journey
Intent success, wrong actions, completion time and handoff recovery matter more than response fluency.
