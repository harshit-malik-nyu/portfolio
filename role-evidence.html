The common thread is trustworthy measurement: establish what the data can support, make the decision explicit, and leave a workflow another person can inspect.

One concrete example: the historical commerce reporting path reconciles 2,756,101 source rows to 2,755,641 distinct events and 17,672 orders. Transaction item rows are not orders, and daily visitor counts cannot be summed into period-unique visitors. Those distinctions change what a report means.

A proposed first-month scope

For a suitable analyst role, agree on one recurring decision and its metric owner. Establish definitions and source access, reconcile a baseline, deliver one usable report, then document exceptions and handoff. Success means the owner can trace the recommendation to reliable inputs and repeat the workflow. Scope and timing depend on access, team priorities and employment arrangements.
