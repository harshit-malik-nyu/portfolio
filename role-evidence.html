A proposed first-month scope

For a suitable analyst role, agree on one recurring decision and its metric owner. Establish definitions and source access, reconcile a baseline, deliver one usable report, then document exceptions and handoff. Success means the owner can trace the recommendation to reliable inputs and repeat the workflow. Scope and timing depend on access, team priorities and employment arrangements.
